## Rejected or deferred evidence

- General heat-exchanger papers or studies that merely report an HTRI comparison are not evidence for HTRI Automation Server fields, calculation internals, or target-version runtime behavior. Only the peer-reviewed unconventional-condenser study above was retained, and solely as bounded support for the non-native-geometry limitation.
- Public vendor descriptions do not replace the licensed Design Manual, an installed-version test, an accepted project model, or the required independent reopen-and-rerun evidence package.
- Member-only TT-31, TT-32, TT-35, and TT-36 were identified as current TechTips, but their bodies were not accessible. No calculation details were adopted from their titles or search snippets.
